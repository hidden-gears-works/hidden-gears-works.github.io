Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who uses these tools

Studio is a set of internal tools. Only its owner uses it, with the owner's own accounts. Nobody else can sign in to it.

Data we access

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API): our own videos, their titles, descriptions, captions and statistics (views, watch time, likes, subscribers gained, traffic sources, audience retention).
  • Meta (Graph API): the list of our Pages, our Instagram professional account and the statistics of our Reels.
  • TikTok (Login Kit, Display API, scopes user.info.basic and video.list): our own account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) and the list of our own videos with their counts (views, likes, comments, shares).

We do not access any personal data of viewers. The statistics we receive are aggregated by the platforms.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, never on a server.
  • The statistics we read may be written to our internal working files.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.

Revoking stops access immediately. On request, we delete the statistics stored in our files.
