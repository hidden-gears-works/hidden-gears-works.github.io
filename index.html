Studio

Studio is a set of internal tools used to prepare, upload and track the videos of our own channels (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok).

These tools are not offered to the public: they only work with our own accounts. They use the platforms' official APIs.

  • YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: uploading our videos as private, updating their descriptions, reading their statistics.
  • Meta Graph API: reading the statistics of our Instagram and Facebook Reels, and preparing or scheduling our own Reels.
  • TikTok Login Kit and Display API: reading the public counts (views, likes, comments, shares) of our own TikTok videos. Nothing is posted to TikTok.
